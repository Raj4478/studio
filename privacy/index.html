Privacy notice

This explains what information I hold about businesses I contact, where it comes from, why I use it, and how to stop me using it. In short: I only use business contact details that businesses publish themselves, I send at most three short emails, and one reply is enough to stop them.

1. Who I am

I'm Rajeshwar, a freelance designer and developer working as a sole trader. I am the controller of the information described here.

2. What this notice covers

It covers the short business emails I send to independent shops and studios offering my services, any replies, and this website.

3. What information I hold

  • Business details: business name, website, town or city, country and type of business.
  • A business email address that your business publishes, such as hello@ or info@, and the page where I found it. If your published address includes a person's name, that is personal data and this notice applies to it.
  • Publicly visible facts about your website that make my email relevant, for example which platform it uses or whether product pages include video.
  • Our correspondence: the emails I send, your replies, and whether you asked me to stop.

I do not buy email lists, guess email addresses, track whether emails are opened, or collect sensitive information.

4. Where it comes from

  • Your own website (its contact, about and product pages).
  • Open public directories such as OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL) and Overture Maps.
  • You, if you reply to me.

5. Why I use it, and my lawful basis

I use it to send a short, relevant offer of my services to your business and to handle your reply. For people in the UK and the EU, my lawful basis is legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR and EU GDPR): promoting my services to businesses they are relevant to. I have weighed that against your interests:

  • I only use addresses businesses publish for business enquiries.
  • Every email is written for that business, about something visible on its own website.
  • I send at most one email and two short follow-ups.
  • Every email says where I found your address and how to stop me.

In the UK I only email companies and LLPs this way, not sole traders or partnerships. I do not send cold emails to businesses in countries whose law requires prior consent for them.

6. How to stop emails, and your right to object

Reply "no" to any email, email singhrajeshwar328@gmail.com with the word "unsubscribe", or see how to stop emails. I act on it the same day, and always within two business days. You have an absolute right to object to direct marketing.

To make sure I never contact you again, I keep a short record of the address or website domain and the date you opted out. My lawful basis for keeping that record is legitimate interests and complying with the law.

7. Who I share it with

I don't sell or rent your information, or share it with anyone for their own marketing. A few service providers process it on my behalf:

  • My email provider (Google), to send and receive email.
  • My website host, which may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security.
  • An AI writing tool (Google's Gemini API), which I may use to help phrase an email. Only publicly available facts about your business are sent to it, never your replies.

I may also disclose information if the law requires it.

8. International transfers

I am based in India, so your information is stored and used in India and by my providers in other countries, including the United States. India does not have a UK or EU "adequacy" decision. I protect the information as described here, and my providers use their own safeguards, such as standard contractual clauses, where these apply.

9. How long I keep it

  • If you don't reply: I delete your details 12 months after my last email.
  • If we talk or work together: for as long as needed for that conversation or project, and invoices for as long as tax law requires.
  • Opt-out records: kept for as long as I send emails, so I never contact you again.

10. Keeping it safe

I keep the minimum information needed, in accounts protected by strong passwords and two-step verification. It is never published.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you are, you can ask me to:

  • give you a copy of your information
  • correct it or delete it
  • restrict how I use it
  • move it to someone else

You can also object at any time. Email singhrajeshwar328@gmail.com and I will reply within one month. There is no charge.

If you're unhappy with how I handle your information, you can complain to a regulator:

  • in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk);
  • in the EU, your local data protection authority;
  • in India, contact me first as the grievance officer; you can then go to the Data Protection Board of India.

12. This website

This website uses no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, no contact forms and no third-party fonts or scripts. If a page shows a video, it is embedded in YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode, which sets no cookies until you play it.

13. Changes

If I change this notice, I'll update the date at the top.
