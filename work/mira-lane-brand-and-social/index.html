Mira Lane: logo, brand kit and social posts

A quiet, premium identity for a handmade silver jewellery brand, and the first three Instagram posts.

Logos · Social media creatives · Concept

Concept project for a fictional brand, made to show how I work. Mira Lane (fictional brand) is not a real client.

The brief

A new jewellery brand has products but no identity. It needs a logo that works on a phone screen, a gift tag and a stamp, plus a consistent look for Instagram from day one.

What I did

  • The icon is a halo with a single stone, so it reads as a ring even at avatar size.
  • Widely spaced serif capitals for a calm, premium feel; a monogram for stamps and packaging.
  • A five-colour palette from silver and skin tones, with one accent (Tide) for calls to action.
  • Three post templates (launch, craft story, carousel tips) that the owner can reuse.

Delivered

  • Primary logo, reversed logo and monogram
  • Colour palette and type pairing
  • Launch post, craft post and carousel cover (1080×1350)
  • Editable templates
