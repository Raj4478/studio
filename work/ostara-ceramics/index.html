Ostara Ceramics: shop website

A calm, fast, mobile-first shop for a small stoneware studio.

Websites · Concept

Concept project for a fictional brand, made to show how I work. Ostara Ceramics (fictional brand) is not a real client.

The brief

A one-person ceramics studio sells through Instagram and markets. It needs a website that feels as handmade as the pots, loads fast on a phone, and makes buying simple.

What I did

  • Warm plaster and clay colours taken from the glazes, with an editorial serif so the site feels crafted rather than templated.
  • Product grid first: name, glaze, price and colour options visible without tapping.
  • Trust details where shoppers look for them: delivery, returns and care in a strip under the hero.
  • Built mobile-first with no heavy libraries: one HTML file, no tracking, nothing slow to load.

Delivered

  • Home page design and build
  • Product grid and product card system
  • Studio story and process sections
  • Mobile, tablet and desktop layouts
  • Newsletter sign-up block
