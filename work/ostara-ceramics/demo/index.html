Our studio

One potter, one wheel, no shortcuts.

Ostara started at a kitchen table in 2019. Today every piece is still thrown, trimmed and glazed by hand, then fired twice in our small studio on the edge of the moors.

We make in small batches, so glazes pool and break a little differently each time. That's the point.

From clay to your table
