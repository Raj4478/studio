Mira Lane: Halo Ring product video

A short product clip for Instagram, product pages and banners, cut from AI-generated footage.

Product videos · Concept

Concept project for a fictional brand, made to show how I work. Mira Lane (fictional brand) is not a real client.

The brief

A small silver jeweller wants a premium clip for each bestseller: the ring turning, light catching the stone, and the brand at the end, in sizes for the Instagram feed and the shop.

What I did

  • Wrote a shot-by-shot brief (lighting, turntable motion, sparkle timing, camera push-in) and generated the footage with an AI video model (Kling).
  • Reframed it into a 4:5 feed cut that keeps the ring in frame as the camera moves in, and a 16:9 cut for product pages and banners.
  • Added captions in the brand fonts so it works with the sound off, then a fade to a clean end card with one call to action.
  • Exported web-ready MP4s (H.264) with cover frames for the grid.

Delivered

  • 7-second 4:5 feed video (1080×1350) with captions and end card
  • 5-second 16:9 clean cut (1920×1080)
  • Cover frames for both
  • Shot brief that can be reused for every product
