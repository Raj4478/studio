Questions

Do I need to send you products?

No. Samples are made from photos already on your website. For full projects we agree what's needed; often your existing photos, or a few clips filmed on a phone, are enough.

What does the free sample involve?

One small piece, for example a 10-second clip or a home page mockup, made for your brand. There's no obligation, and you don't need to get on a call.

How do we work across time zones?

I work Monday to Friday, 9:00–19:00 India time, which overlaps UK mornings and early mornings on the US East Coast. Most projects run entirely over email.

How does payment work?

Each project has a fixed price agreed in writing first. Payment is by international bank transfer or PayPal: half to start and half on delivery for larger projects.

Who owns the work?

You do. Once a project is paid, you get full rights to use the final files for your business.
